[ ABOUT ME ]

MADE IN COLOMBIA
BASED IN MADRID.

Outside design, you'll probably find me walking around a new city, taking photos of random details, building LEGO, cooking something, at a concert, travelling or simply enjoying some time on my own. Music, photography, cinema, objects, people and spontaneous everyday moments constantly find their way into how I see and think about design.

I don't believe everything needs to be taken too seriously. Design should never be boring. It can be simple and useful while still having character, making people curious and, hopefully, bringing a little joy along the way.

The Master’s in Design strengthened my ability to approach design through research, strategy, and visual communication. It expanded my perspective beyond product development, allowing me to explore how design can respond to complex problems, communicate ideas clearly, and create meaningful experiences.

Curious by nature, a little obsessed with details, and always interested in understanding why things work the way they do and how they could work better.

My background is in Industrial Design, but I've never really stayed in just one side of design. I've moved between physical and digital products, visual design, 3D, branding and, eventually, UX/UI and digital experiences. It wasn't a perfectly planned path more a mix of curiosity, experimentation and following the things that genuinely interested me.

What I enjoy most is seeing an idea become real. That moment when a thought becomes a sketch, a sketch becomes a concept, and eventually something you can see, touch or interact with. I like experimenting, questioning, refining and finding that little something that gives each project its own personality.

Approach
I connect research, structure and visual craft to shape digital products that feel considered from the first interaction to the final detail.

Perspective
My industrial design background brings systems thinking, material sensitivity and a human point of view to every interface and brand experience.

[EXP.]

Almost eight years of designing, learning, changing perspectives and figuring things out along the way.

My journey has taken me through different sides of design, from physical products, packaging and visual communication to branding, e-commerce, UX/UI and digital products. Each role has brought a different way of thinking, new people to learn from and new problems to solve.

Working alongside developers, marketing teams, production, sales, content and other creatives has taught me that good design rarely happens in isolation. It comes from listening, asking the right questions, understanding different perspectives and connecting the dots.

Over time, I’ve learned not only how to design things, but how to understand the systems, people and decisions behind them. Today, I bring all those experiences together in my work as a Product Designer, creating digital experiences for B2B and e-commerce while still carrying with me everything I learned from designing beyond the screen.

Different roles, different disciplines, different people,
all part of the same journey.
